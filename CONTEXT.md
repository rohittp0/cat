# Varsha's game

A cute arcade game that opens directly into play, designed for phones first.

## Language

**Varsha**:
The game's cat. Her name is fixed. Players guide her across the play area to catch falling treats.

**Play area**:
The space where Varsha walks or runs toward a tapped or dragged destination. Arrow keys and on-screen directional controls also move her.

**Treats**:
Falling fish, yarn, and hearts. Catching them earns points.

**Round**:
A 45-second game. Players can pause or restart it, or play again after it ends.

**Score**:
Points earned during the current round.

**Best score**:
The highest round score saved in the current browser.
